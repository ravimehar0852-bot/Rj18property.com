Who We Are

A Decade of Trusted Property Deals in Jhunjhunu & Sikar

RJ18 Property Dealer & Work Service was founded on a simple idea — that buying or selling a property should feel safe, clear and fair. What began as a small local dealership has grown into one of the most trusted property names across Jhunjhunu and Sikar, built entirely on referrals from happy clients.

Every plot, house or commercial space we list is personally verified for legal clarity before it reaches a client. Our consultants know these localities street by street — which helps you make a decision with confidence, not guesswork.

  • ✓ Complete transparency on pricing and paperwork
  • ✓ Experienced consultants with deep local knowledge
  • ✓ In-house documentation & registration support
  • ✓ Long-term relationships built on honesty, not one-time sales
